Hello! I am an undergraduate Industrial Engineering student at the University of Toronto, specializing in AI/ML.

My core interest lies in applying robust AI/ML, operations research, and optimization techniques to create intelligent solutions for embodied AI and power grids. 

Currently, I’m developing novel machine learning approaches for handling strict, complex constraints in power systems as a research student with Prof. <a href="https://priyadonti.com/" target="_blank">Priya Donti</a> (MIT <a href="https://www.eecs.mit.edu/" target="_blank">EECS</a> & <a href="https://lids.mit.edu/" target="_blank">LIDS</a>). At UofT, I have worked with Profs. <a href="https://mohajerinesfahani.github.io/index.html" target="_blank">Peyman Esfahani</a> (<a href="https://www.mie.utoronto.ca/" target="_blank">UofT MIE</a>, <a href="https://www.tudelft.nl/en/me/about/departments/delft-center-for-systems-and-control/">Delft Center for Systems and Control</a>) and <a href="https://d3m.mie.utoronto.ca/members/ssanner/" target="_blank">Scott Sanner</a> (UofT MIE/CS, <a href="https://vectorinstitute.ai/" target="_blank">Vector Institute</a>) on imitation learning via inverse optimization, as well as with Prof. <a href="https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~florian/" target="_blank">Florian Shkurti</a> (<a href="https://vectorinstitute.ai/" target="_blank">Vector Institute</a>, UofT Robotics Institute) on perception algorithms implementation. During the summer of 2025, I also worked as an AI intern at <a href="https://rocketinnovationstudio.ca/" target="_blank">Rocket Innovation Studio</a>, building a transparent resume-screening platform for <a href="https://rocketmortgage.ca/" target="_blank">Rocket Mortgage</a>.

I am driven by the challenge of designing safe and efficient intelligent systems for positive impact. If you are interested in collaborating or connecting, please feel free to reach out!
